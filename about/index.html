About The Metro Ledger

A national daily newspaper published in Washington, D.C., since 1963.

The Newspaper

The Metro Ledger is an independent daily newspaper serving readers in all fifty states. Founded in 1963, the Ledger covers science, technology, transportation, and public affairs with a reporting staff dedicated to accuracy, clarity, and independence. The paper publishes seven editions a week in print and continuously online.

Editorial Standards

The Ledger reports with verified facts and named sources wherever possible. Editors and reporters correct errors promptly; corrections appear in a standing place in the paper and are attached to the affected articles online. Reporting is never traded for advertising, and advertisers exercise no influence over news coverage. Opinion appears on the opinion pages and is clearly labeled.
